XEERARKA GOOBTA REGENCY COURT

Hawlgallada soo socda si ADAG ayaa looga mamnuucay Regency Court:

1. Ciyaaraha kubbadda, wadooyinka baaskiillada / skuutarada / skateboard‑ka ama wax kasta oo kale oo awood aadanaha ku shaqeeya laguma oggola gudaha Regency Court ama meelaha guud ee dhismayaasha.
2. Alaabta shaqsiga ah ee lagu daayo meelaha guud (gudaha ama dibadda) waa la qaadi doonaa iyada oo aan wax digniin ah la bixin, kharashkuna waxa uu ku dhici doonaa qofka iska leh alaabtaas.
3. Isu-ururinta carruurta, dhallinyarada ama dadka waaweyn meel kasta oo Regency Court ah waa mamnuuc (marka laga reebo guryaha gaarka ah). Tani waxa ay ku jirtaa dhammaan meelaha guud, jaranjarooyinka, baarkinka, beero, darbiyada xayndaabka, dhismayaasha dibadda, goobaha qashinka iyo albaabbada.
4. Fadhiisashada darbiyada, xayndaabyada, geedaha, cawska, duurka, sariiraha ubaxa, jaranjarooyinka ama talaabooyinka waa mamnuuc.
5. Dabeecad qaylo badan, fowdo ah, aflagaado, gardarro, cabsi‑gelin, bulsho‑ka‑soo horjeed ah, ixtiraam‑darro ama dabeecad kale oo dhib ku ah dadka kale lagama oggola carruurta, dhallinyarada ama dadka waaweyn.
6. Burburinta, sawirada darbiyada (graffiti), ku dhejinta warqadaha sharci darrada ah, tuurista qashinka ama wasakhowga kale waa mamnuuc.
7. Isticmaalka khamriga ama daroogooyinka goobaha dadweynaha ama meelaha guud ee Regency Court si adag ayaa loo mamnuucay.
8. Dharka lama sudhin karo balakoonada, lagama soo bixin karo daaqadaha ama laguma ridi karo meel uu qof kale arki karo.
9. Deggeneyaashu waa inay ixtiraamaan xuquuqda deriskooda ee ku saabsan nolol degan oo deggan, isla markaana ay u dhaqmaan si ay u kobciyaan xiriir wanaagsan.
10. Milkiilaha guri kiro ku jira (Leaseholder) ayaa mas’uul ka ah wixii waxyeello ah ee kiraystayaashiisu ka geystaan meelaha guud.
11. Dayactirka ama hagaajinta gaadiidka — oo ay ku jirto dhaqiddoodu — laguma oggola gudaha goobta.
12. Haddii qawaaniintan la jebiyo, degganaha waxa laga saari doonaa Regency Court, qofkii laga saarana dib looma soo celin doono.
13. Isticmaalka khaldan ee bacaha qashinka waa mamnuuc. Looguma talagalin in lagu shubo qashin aan u qalmin. Haddii aad hubin la’dahay, weydii shaqaalaha.
14. Qashinka waaweyn (Bulky Waste) waa in lagu tuuraa si waafaqsan habraaca rasmiga ah. Alaabta guriga ama waxyaabaha waaweyn lagama tagi karo bannaanka. Haddii aad shaki qabto, la hadal shaqaalaha.
15. DHAMMAAN albaabbada meelaha guud waa in mar walba la XIRAA, lagumana oggola in la furo sabab kasta ha ahaatee.
16. Sariiraha ubaxa looma isticmaali karo meel lagu kaydiyo waxyaabaha degganeyaasha.
17. Haddii qaadista baaskiil, gaari‑carruur ama shay kale oo jaranjarooyinka lagu qaado ay waxyeello u gaysato darbiyada ama sagxadaha, shirkadda maamulku waxay xaq u leedahay inay kharashka dayactirka ka qaaddo degganaha.
18. Xayawaannada guriga lagu hayo laguma oggola guryaha.
19. Waxaa mamnuuc ah in la quudiyo bisadaha, eeyaha ama xayawaannada kale ee duurjoogta ah.
